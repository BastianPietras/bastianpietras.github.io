I am an applied mathematician and computational neuroscientist studying how interactions among neurons give rise to collective dynamics and computation. I develop exact and reduced models to understand how synchronization, adaptation and synaptic coupling shape the activity of neuronal populations.

My emerging research direction connects these models to whole-brain dynamics and the interactions among brain, body and environment. I aim to build interpretable multiscale models that can be tested against neural, physiological and behavioural observations.
